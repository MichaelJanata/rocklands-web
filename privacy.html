Rocklands — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information the Rocklands app ("we," "us," or "our") collects, why, and how it's handled, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

Rocklands is operated by Michael Janata ("Rocklands," "we," "us," or "our"), based in South Africa. This policy applies to the Rocklands mobile app and the website rocklandsapp.co.za.

2. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password in readable form).

Purchases. If you start the free trial or buy the full version, Apple sends us a record of that purchase: what was bought, when, and Apple's transaction number. We use it only to unlock the full version on your account. We never see your payment details.

Location data. With your permission, the app uses your device's location while you're using it, to show your position on the map. Approved contributors also use it to record the GPS coordinates of new boulders they add. The app doesn't use your location in the background, and we don't store your position unless you're a contributor saving a boulder's location.

Photos. If you're an approved contributor, the app accesses your camera or photo library, with your permission, to let you photograph boulders and routes or import existing photos. Photos you add are uploaded to become part of the guide.

Invitations. Contributors can invite each other by email to work on an unpublished area together. When you're invited, we use your email address to find your account and to send you the invitation.

Device permissions. The app also requests access to your device's motion sensor, purely to detect when you turn your phone sideways so the topo viewer can open in fullscreen landscape mode — this reading isn't stored or transmitted anywhere.

On your device only. Places you pin on the map, your settings and the downloaded guide are stored on your phone and aren't sent to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account, and control what you can see and do based on your role;
  • Let approved contributors add boulders, problems, and photos to the guide, accurately located, and work on them together;
  • Unlock the full version after a trial or purchase made through Apple;
  • Send account emails: sign-up confirmation, password reset, and invitations;
  • Show weather forecasts for fixed points in Rocklands;
  • Maintain and improve the app, and investigate misuse, including the scraping and redistribution restrictions in our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we don't build in any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools of our own. The only usage data collected is the map provider's anonymous telemetry, described in Section 4.

4. Who We Share It With

We share information only with the services that make the app work, never for advertising or resold to third parties:

  • Supabase — our backend provider, which stores your account details, your purchase record, and the guide's content.
  • Cloudflare — delivers the guide, photos, and maps to your phone. Like any web server, it sees your device's IP address when you download.
  • Apple — processes your trial and purchase; we never see your payment details.
  • Resend — sends our account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset, invitations) to your email address.
  • Mapbox — provides the map and satellite imagery; your device requests map data from Mapbox directly. Mapbox's map software also collects anonymous usage and location data for its own analytics and to improve its maps. This data isn't linked to your Rocklands account and isn't used for advertising. You can switch it off from the ⓘ button on the map ("Mapbox Telemetry").
  • The Norwegian Meteorological Institute (met.no) — supplies weather forecasts; the app asks for forecasts for fixed points in Rocklands, never your own location or account details.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they handle the data that passes through them.

5. Data Retention and Deleting Your Account

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account, your profile, and your purchase record from our systems straight away. Purchases themselves stay with your Apple ID, so if you create a new account you can get the full version back with Restore purchases.

Accounts of approved contributors can't be deleted in the app, because the guide content they added is linked to them; contact us and we'll delete your personal information by hand. Content you've contributed to the shared guide — such as route photos or descriptions — may remain part of the guide after your account is deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to the exceptions in Section 5;
  • Object to how we process your information;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe we've handled your information unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

7. Children's Privacy

Rocklands is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Our service providers, including Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, and Mapbox, may process and store data on servers outside South Africa. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information continues to receive a level of protection consistent with POPIA.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls that restrict who can see and edit different kinds of content, and encrypted connections between the app and our backend. No system is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect a new feature or a change in the law. If we make a material change, we'll let you know inside the app or by email before it takes effect.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise your rights under Section 6? Contact our Information Officer at support@rocklandsapp.co.za.
