Rocklands — Support

Need help with the Rocklands app, found a wrong grade or a boulder in the wrong place, or have a question about your purchase? Email us at support@rocklandsapp.co.za and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email address. We'll send you a code to set a new one. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

I bought the full version but it's locked again

Open Settings → Full version and tap Restore purchases. Your purchase belongs to your Apple ID, so make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How does the free trial work?

The 3-day trial unlocks the full version. When it ends, the app keeps working with the free features, and you can buy the full version once from Settings → Full version. There's no subscription.

Can I use the app without signal?

Yes. Rocklands is built for areas with no reception: the guide and photos are downloaded to your phone, and with the full version the offline maps too. Open the app once on Wi-Fi before your trip and let the downloads finish (you can follow them in Settings).

Something in the guide is wrong

Email us the area, boulder, and problem name, and what should change. A photo helps.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and personal details straight away. See our Privacy Policy for details.
